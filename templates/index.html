3D STL Viewer

by @zrecore · github.com/zrecore/3d-viewer-golang

3D controls

Orbit
Mouse drag with the left button
Touch drag with one finger
Pan
Mouse Shift + drag, or drag with the right or middle button
Touch drag with two fingers
Zoom
Mouse scroll the wheel
Touch pinch with two fingers; it zooms toward your fingers
Twist
Touch turn two fingers around each other to spin the model
Coast
Flick an orbit or pan drag and let go: the model keeps moving, then slows to a stop. Press, touch or scroll to stop it sooner.
Reset view
Mouse the Reset view button in the toolbar
Touch the Reset view button, or double-tap the model

Models

Use Upload in the toolbar to open an .stl file from your computer or device. The last model you open is kept only in this browser and shown again next time. Nothing is sent to a server.

Toolbar

Drag the ⋯ strip to move the toolbar or this window. The arrow button at the top of the toolbar collapses it to icons.
